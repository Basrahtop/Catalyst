# Investor Guide

## Getting Started

Welcome to Catalyst! This guide will help you start investing in African SMEs and earn returns through our profit-sharing model.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit the Investor Dashboard at `https://investor.catalyst.com`
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Fill in your details:
   - Email address
   - Password (min 8 characters)
   - Full name
   - Phone number
   - Select **"Investor"** as your role
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete KYC verification (upload ID document)

## Step 2: Fund Your Wallet

### Option A: Nigerian Naira (NGN)

1. Navigate to **"Wallet"** → **"Deposit"**
2. Choose amount to deposit
3. Complete bank transfer using provided details
4. Funds appear in your wallet within 5-30 minutes

### Option B: Cardano (ADA)

1. Install a Cardano wallet:
   - [Nami Wallet](https://namiwallet.io)
   - [Eternl Wallet](https://eternl.io)
   - [Flint Wallet](https://flint-wallet.com)
2. Purchase ADA from an exchange (Binance, Coinbase, etc.)
3. Transfer ADA to your wallet
4. You're ready to invest directly from your wallet!

### Option C: USDM Stablecoin

Similar to ADA, but using USDM for dollar-denominated exposure.

## Step 3: Browse Investment Opportunities

1. Click **"Explore Opportunities"**
2. Use filters to find SMEs that match your criteria:
   - **Location**: Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, etc.
   - **Industry**: Fashion, Food, Technology, etc.
   - **Trust Score**: Minimum reputation threshold
   - **Funding Amount**: Investment size range
   - **Term Length**: 3, 6, or 12 months

## Step 4: Review SME Details

Click on any opportunity to see:

- **Business Information**: What they sell, their story
- **Trust Score Breakdown**: Platform trust, marketplace performance, customer feedback, financial history
- **Funding Details**: Amount needed, profit share %, term length
- **Use of Funds**: How they'll use your investment
- **Previous Performance**: Past profit reports (if available)

## Step 5: Make Your Investment

### NGN Investment:

1. Click **"Invest Now"**
2. Enter investment amount (minimum ₦1.00)
3. Review terms and profit share percentage
4. Click **"Confirm Investment"**
5. Funds deduct from your wallet
6. You'll receive email confirmation

### ADA/USDM Investment:

1. Click **"Invest with Crypto"**
2. Enter amount in ADA or USDM
3. System shows NGN-equivalent value
4. Click **"Prepare Transaction"**
5. Your wallet app opens with transaction details
6. Review and **sign transaction** in your wallet
7. Return to Catalyst and click **"Confirm"**
8. Enter blockchain transaction hash
9. Investment confirmed within 5-10 minutes

## Step 6: Track Your Portfolio

Navigate to **"My Investments"** to see:

- **Active Investments**: Ongoing funding requests
- **Total Invested**: Across all currencies
- **Expected Returns**: Based on profit share %
- **Payment Schedule**: When to expect profit distributions
- **Performance**: How each SME is performing

## Step 7: Receive Profit Distributions

When SMEs report profits:

1. You'll receive **email notification**
2. Your share calculates automatically based on contribution %
3. Profits deposit to your wallet (NGN, ADA, or USDM)
4. You can **reinvest** or **withdraw**

### Example Calculation:

- You invested: ₦100,000 (20% of ₦500,000 request)
- SME reports: ₦1,000,000 in profits
- Profit share: 20%
- Your gross share: ₦1,000,000 × 20% × 20% = ₦40,000
- Platform fee (10%): ₦4,000
- **Your net profit: ₦36,000** (36% return!)

## Step 8: Withdraw Funds

To withdraw from your wallet:

1. Navigate to **"Wallet"** → **"Withdraw"**
2. Select currency (NGN, ADA, USDM)
3. Enter amount and destination:
   - **NGN**: Bank account details
   - **ADA/USDM**: Your wallet address
4. Confirm withdrawal
5. Funds arrive within:
   - NGN: 1-24 hours
   - ADA/USDM: 5-20 minutes (blockchain confirmation)

## Understanding Risk

### What Protects Your Investment?

1. **Trust Scores**: Only vetted SMEs with proven performance or Agent sponsorship
2. **Profit Sharing**: You only earn when SMEs profit (aligned incentives)
3. **Blockchain Escrow**: Crypto investments locked in smart contracts
4. **Risk Mitigation Fund**: 10% of platform revenue reserves for defaults
5. **Diversification**: Spread investments across multiple SMEs

### What Are The Risks?

- SMEs may report **lower profits** than expected
- Some SMEs may **default** or fail
- **Exchange rate risk** for crypto investors (ADA, USDM)
- **Platform risk**: Catalyst is early-stage technology

**Recommendation**: Start with small amounts (₦10,000-50,000) across multiple SMEs to learn the system and diversify risk.

## Best Practices

### Diversify Your Portfolio

Don't invest all funds in one SME. Spread across:
- 5-10 different businesses
- Different industries
- Different locations
- Various term lengths

### Review Trust Scores

Higher trust scores indicate:
- Proven track record
- Verified business operations
- Positive customer feedback
- Reliable payment history

### Start Small

- Begin with ₦10,000-50,000 per SME
- Learn the system
- Build confidence
- Scale up gradually

### Monitor Performance

Check your dashboard weekly to:
- Track profit reports
- See SME updates
- Identify top performers
- Adjust strategy
