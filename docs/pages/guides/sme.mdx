# SME Guide

## Welcome to Catalyst!

This guide will help you access capital for your business and manage your funding relationship with investors.

## Step 1: Create Your SME Account

1. Visit `https://sme.catalyst.com`
2. Click **"Register"**
3. Fill in your details:
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Full name
   - Business name
   - Phone number
   - Select **"SME"** as your role
4. Verify your email

## Step 2: Complete Your Profile

A strong profile increases trust and investment likelihood:

### Business Information
- **Business Name**: Your registered/trading name
- **Industry**: Fashion, Food, Technology, etc.
- **Location**: City/state
- **Year Started**: When you launched
- **Description**: What you sell, your story (2-3 paragraphs)

### Verification Documents
Upload clear photos/scans of:
- Government-issued ID (NIN, Driver's License, Passport)
- Business Registration (if registered)
- Bank Account Statement (last 3 months)
- Shop/office photos

**Tip**: Complete profiles with verification get 40% more investor interest!

## Step 3: Build Your Trust Score

Your trust score (0-1,000 points) determines investment attractiveness:

### Platform Trust (300 pts)
- Complete KYC verification: +100 pts
- Upload business documents: +75 pts
- Verify bank account: +50 pts
- Complete profile fully: +40 pts
- Platform tenure (time): +35 pts

### Marketplace Performance (350 pts)
- Connect existing online sales (Instagram, WhatsApp Business)
- Link marketplace accounts (Jumia, Konga)
- Demonstrate sales volume and fulfillment

### Customer Feedback (150 pts)
- Import reviews from existing platforms
- Encourage customers to leave feedback
- Respond quickly to inquiries

### Financial History (200 pts)
- Successfully complete funding cycles
- Report profits accurately
- Make payments on time

**New to business?** Consider the Human Trust pathway with Agent sponsorship (see Step 4).

## Step 4: Choose Your Path

### Path A: Performance Trust

Best if you have:
- Existing sales history (Instagram, WhatsApp, marketplace)
- Customer reviews and testimonials
- Proven track record (even if informal)

**Action**: Connect your business accounts to import transaction data and build your score automatically.

### Path B: Human Trust (Agent Sponsorship)

Best if you:
- Have a solid business plan but no sales yet
- Are a youth entrepreneur starting out
- Lack formal business history

**Action**: Find an Agent to sponsor you:
1. Click **"Find Agents"**
2. Browse Agents in your area/industry
3. Submit sponsorship request with your business plan
4. If accepted, Agent provides trust boost (150-200 pts)

**Agent Benefits**: They earn 5% commission on your profits, so they're motivated to support your success!

## Step 5: Create a Funding Request

1. Click **"Create Funding Request"**
2. Fill in details:

### Basic Information
- **Purpose**: What do you need funding for?
  - Inventory purchase
  - Equipment
  - Working capital
  - Business expansion
- **Amount**: How much? (₦100,000 - ₦5,000,000)
- **Term Length**: 3, 6, or 12 months
- **Profit Share %**: What % of profits will you share? (15-30% typical)

### Detailed Description
Explain:
- Current business situation
- Why you need this funding
- How you'll use the money
- Expected outcomes
- Timeline for results

### Financial Projections
Estimate monthly revenues and profits:
- Current monthly revenue: ₦X
- Expected revenue with funding: ₦Y
- Estimated monthly profit: ₦Z

**Tip**: Be realistic! Investors appreciate honest projections over overly optimistic ones.

### Accepted Currencies
Choose which currencies you'll accept:
- ☑️ Nigerian Naira (NGN)
- ☑️ Cardano (ADA)
- ☑️ USDM Stablecoin

Most SMEs accept all three to maximize investor pool.

3. Submit for review
4. Admin reviews within 1-3 business days
5. If approved, your request goes live!

## Step 6: Attract Investors

Once live, investors can discover and fund your request:

### Tips to Attract Investment:
1. **Update your profile regularly** with business news
2. **Respond to investor questions** quickly
3. **Share your funding request** on social media
4. **Provide evidence** of business operations (photos, testimonials)
5. **Engage with the community** (comment on forums, share insights)

### Partial Funding
Investors can contribute any amount. Your request shows:
- Target: ₦500,000
- Funded: ₦200,000 (40%)
- Remaining: ₦300,000

Once 100% funded, status changes to **APPROVED** and funds release to your wallet!

## Step 7: Receive Your Funds

When fully funded:

1. You receive **email notification**
2. Funds appear in your wallet
3. Payment schedule generates automatically
4. You can **withdraw to bank account**:
   - Navigate to "Wallet" → "Withdraw"
   - Enter bank details
   - Funds arrive in 1-24 hours

**For Crypto Investments**: ADA/USDM goes to your Cardano wallet directly from escrow smart contract.

## Step 8: Use Funds Wisely

Your success = investor returns + your growth!

### Best Practices:
- **Use funds as stated** in your funding request
- **Keep records** of purchases/expenses
- **Track sales and profits** carefully
- **Communicate with investors** about progress
- **Reinvest profits** for continued growth

## Step 9: Report Profits

At each installment due date:

1. You'll receive **reminder notification**
2. Navigate to **"My Funding"** → **"Report Profit"**
3. Enter total profits for the period
4. System calculates **4-Way Split** automatically:
   - Investors get their profit share %
   - You keep the majority (60-70%)
   - Agent gets 5% commission (if sponsored)
   - Platform takes service fee (16-18%)

### Example: ₦1M in Profits

Assume 20% profit share, ₦500K funded, Agent sponsored:

- **Investors**: ₦180,000 (18%)
- **You (SME)**: ₦600,000 (60%)
- **Agent**: ₦40,000 (4%)
- **Platform**: ₦162,000 (16.2%)
- **Risk Fund**: ₦18,000 (1.8%)

**You keep ₦600,000 to reinvest and grow!**

5. Funds automatically distribute from your wallet
6. Process repeats next installment

## Step 10: Complete Your Term

After all installments paid:

1. **Funding request marks "COMPLETED"**
2. Your **Financial History score increases**
3. You can create **new funding requests** with:
   - Higher amounts
   - Better terms
   - Faster approval

Successful completion = easier future funding!

## Managing Challenges

### What if profits are lower than expected?

Be honest! Report actual profits. The profit-sharing model means:
- Lower profits = lower payments (investors understand business ups/downs)
- Better to report accurately than default
- Communicate reasons (seasonal slump, unexpected costs)

### What if you can't pay an installment?

Contact support immediately:
- Explain the situation
- Propose payment plan
- Work with platform to find solutions
- **Don't ghost investors!**

Missing payments hurts your Financial History score but can be recovered with good communication.
